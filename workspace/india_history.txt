1. 1947 - Independence of India, Integration of princely states, Jawaharlal Nehru appointed as the first Prime Minister of Independent India
2. 1948 - The assassination of Mahatma Gandhi, India-Pakistan war on Kashmir
3. 1949 - Indian Constituent Assembly adopts new constitution
4. 1950 - India becomes a Republic and the Constitution of India comes into force
5. 1951 - First amendment, First five-year plan
6. 1952 - First general election of India, Panchayati Raj established
7. 1954 - Indo-Chinese Panchsheel Treaty
8. 1955 - The Imperial Bank of India was changed into the State Bank of India
9. 1958 - Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act implemented
10. 1961 - Indian troops annexed the Portuguese territories of Goa, Daman, and Diu
11. 1962 - The Sino- Indian war breaks out which ends in a decisive victory for China
12. 1965 - India-Pakistan war breaks out, Hindi becomes the official language of India
13. 1966 - Tashkent conference
14. 1969 - Nationalization of Banks
15. 1971 - India-Pakistan war and the liberation of Bangladesh. Simla Agreement was signed on July 2nd
16. 1973 - Establishment of 1st International Telephone Exchange in India
17. 1974 - First nuclear test at Pokhran
18. 1975 - State of emergency, Aryabhatta (India’s first satellite) was launched, 20 point program
19. 1984 - Bhopal gas tragedy
20. 1988 - Creation of Securities Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
21. 1991 - Economic reforms, Devaluation of the Rupee
22. 2000 - Formation of new states of Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, and Uttranchal
23. 2002 - Gujarat riots
24. 2004 - The tsunami caused massive devastation around the Southern and Eastern coasts of India
25. 2006 - The US and India signed a nuclear agreement
26. 2008 - Moon mission - Chandrayaan-1 launched on October 22
27. 2013 - Mars Orbiter Mission launched
28. 2016 - India becomes a member of MTCR
29. 2017 - Goods and Services Tax (GST) was implemented
30. 2019 - The abrogation of Article 370 and 35A which granted special status to the state of Jammu and Kashmir
31. 2020 - On March 25, the Indian government declared a complete lockdown of the nation due to an increase in Covid-19 cases
32. 2021 - Authorization of two COVID-19 vaccines, Covaxin and Covershield, in emergency situations
